# Case: Nonsense — downloadable sound effects

All included source recordings are CC0. You may reuse, edit and redistribute them, including commercially. Source credits are retained as a courtesy.

- Kenney, **RPG Audio**: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
  Footsteps, opening doors, creaks, metal latch, paper and cloth. Original license included.
- Kenney, **Voiceover Pack**: https://kenney.nl/assets/voiceover-pack
  Voice credits and license included. `mumble-*` are filtered, softened versions of the male/female “ready” recordings, used as incidental indistinct speech; `hurry` is the original warning. These are incidental voices, not a fully voiced script.
- rubberduck, **40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX**: https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx
  Water ambience, rain and splash recordings. CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## Add your own
Replace matching `.ogg` files in `audio/sfx/`, or add filenames in `sound-bank.js` under `SOUND_FILES`. Keep files short and normalized below clipping. Use CC0 or check the license before redistributing someone else's recording. Sound remains exclusive to Ears, as required by the game's sense rules. Unmapped effects retain the procedural audio fallback.
